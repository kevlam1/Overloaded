# Wizards of the Coast Fan Content Policy (read 2026-10-26)

Source: `company.wizards.com/en/legal/fancontentpolicy`, fetched once and read **through a summarizing tool**, not by eye. The page shows "Last Updated: November 15, 2017" and nothing on it says it is the current version. **Read the real page in a browser before the site goes public**, and copy the disclaimer from it exactly.

## What it says (as summarized)
- **Free access:** you can't require "payments, surveys, downloads, subscriptions, or email registration" to use Fan Content. Sponsorships, ads and donations are allowed. You can't sell or license it. A login to play a private group game is allowed.
- **Verbatim copying:** Fan Content "does not include the verbatim copying and reposting of Wizards' IP" (example: counterfeit or proxy cards). Wizards' IP is defined to include cards, pictures and gameplay.
- **Other games:** "Don't use Wizards' IP in other games. This includes your own or other people's games or game components." Nothing is said about virtual tabletops or deck-playing sites.
- **Logos and trademarks:** don't use Wizards' logos and trademarks without prior written permission. The page's list of excluded images includes mana symbols.
- **Stopping you:** "We have the right to stop or restrict your use of Wizards' IP at any time, for any reason or no reason." Wizards' Terms of Use and Code of Conduct also apply.
- **Required disclaimer:** "[Title of your Fan Content] is unofficial Fan Content permitted under the Fan Content Policy. Not approved/endorsed by Wizards. Portions of the materials used are property of Wizards of the Coast. ©Wizards of the Coast LLC."

## What it means for Overloaded
- **Fits:** free, no payment, no email registration, private lobbies only, no accounts, and Scryfall's own terms say its data and images are provided under this policy "for the primary purpose of creating additional Magic software".
- **Kevin's judgment (2026-10-26):** probably fine, since Moxfield has its own playtest feature. That shows the practice exists, but it is not Wizards' permission for us, and Moxfield may have terms we don't know about.
- **Gray areas, not settled by the page:** whether a virtual tabletop counts as using Wizards' IP "in other games", whether showing full card images counts as "verbatim copying and reposting" (Scryfall hosts them, we link to them), and the mana symbols.
- **Rules we follow regardless:** show the disclaimer (with "Overloaded" as the title) on every public page and in the README; never charge or require sign-up; don't use Wizards' logos or the Magic logo; don't use "Magic: The Gathering" in the site's title or name (the project name is Overloaded); mana symbols only as they appear inside Scryfall's card images, and if we draw our own, check this first; leave card images unaltered (also Scryfall's rule).
- **Real risk:** Wizards can ask us to stop at any time. For a class project the cost is low; the plan for it is to take the site down if asked.
- **Not asked of Wizards:** we have not contacted them and don't need to.
