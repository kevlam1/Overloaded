# Fonts

Self-hosted (the site's content policy allows fonts only from its own address). Added 2026-11-24 for the redesign, approved by Kevin.

| File | Font | License | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `inter-latin.woff2` | Inter, variable weight 400 to 700, latin subset | SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`OFL-inter.txt`) | Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com, Inter v20); project: https://github.com/rsms/inter |
| `barlow-condensed-600-latin.woff2`, `barlow-condensed-700-latin.woff2` | Barlow Condensed SemiBold and Bold, latin subset | SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`OFL-barlow-condensed.txt`) | Google Fonts (fonts.gstatic.com, Barlow Condensed v13); project: https://github.com/jpt/barlow |

The OFL allows using, bundling and redistributing the fonts with the site, free of charge, as long as the license travels with them and the fonts are not sold on their own.
